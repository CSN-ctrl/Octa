# Chaos Star Universe - Sarakt Genesis World (WaaS)

## 1. Overview

Chaos Star Universe is the overarching universe framework. Sarakt is its Genesis world: a World-as-a-Service (WaaS) environment where users own, build, and control evolving digital systems. It combines ownership infrastructure, AI-driven populations, and dynamic economies inside a persistent, scalable universe.

Unlike traditional games or virtual environments, Sarakt is designed as a living system where worlds evolve over time, economies emerge naturally, and users can scale their influence from individual land ownership to full control of solar systems within Chaos Star Universe.

## 2. Core Concept

Sarakt operates on two primary axes:

### A. Ownership Scale

Users can own and control different layers of the universe:

- Land - individual plots within a world
- Cities - clusters of land with governance and economic control
- Planets - entire worlds with customizable rules and resources
- Solar Systems - collections of planets with interplanetary economies

### B. Civilization Evolution

Each world evolves through structured stages:

1. Origin Stage
   - Cellular life emerges
   - Environmental systems form
2. Biological Stage
   - Flora and fauna develop
   - Ecosystems stabilize
3. Sentient Stage
   - Intelligent species emerge
   - Early social structures form
4. Civilization Stage
   - Cities, trade, governance
   - Organized economies
5. Technological Stage
   - Industry and automation
   - Advanced infrastructure
6. Space Stage
   - Orbital systems
   - Interplanetary expansion

## 3. World Structure

Each world in Sarakt is defined by four key components:

### 1. Resources

- Unique to each world
- Cannot be replicated elsewhere
- Form the foundation of economic activity

Examples:

- Raw materials
- Biological compounds
- Advanced synthetic elements

### 2. Race (Dominant Species)

Each world develops a primary intelligent species that defines:

- Behavior patterns
- Economic strengths
- Social structures

Traits may include:

- Learning speed
- Productivity
- Loyalty
- Adaptability

### 3. Environment

- Climate and terrain
- Resource distribution
- Habitability conditions

Environment directly impacts:

- Resource generation
- Population growth
- Infrastructure requirements

### 4. Ruleset

Defined at higher ownership levels (planet/system owners):

- Economic policies
- Resource flow rules
- Development constraints

## 4. Economic System

Sarakt's economy is designed around scarcity, production, and dependency.

Core principles:

- Resources are limited and world-specific
- Production requires multiple stages
- Worlds depend on each other

Economic flow:

Land -> Resource Extraction -> Refinement -> Production -> Trade -> Profit

Interplanetary economy:

- No world is self-sufficient
- Trade between planets is required
- Logistics introduces:
  - Cost
  - Time
  - Risk

Currency structure:

- Universe Reserve Currency (`xBGL`) - reserve unit across the Chaos Star Universe economy
- Secondary tokens - used for specialized systems
- Private/shadow layer - optional anonymous transactions

## 5. AI Population System

NPCs are not static entities. They are dynamic participants in the economy.

Characteristics:

- Spawn without predefined personalities
- Learn behaviors over time
- Adapt based on environment and ownership

Core mechanics:

- NPCs seek employment
- Gain skills through work
- Develop loyalty to owners

Loyalty system:

- High loyalty -> long-term retention
- Maximum loyalty -> integration into owner's influence

Behavioral outcomes:

- Efficient economies under good management
- Instability under poor conditions
- Emergent social structures

## 6. Ownership System

Ownership is tied to function, not just possession.

### Land Ownership

- Resource generation
- Building placement
- Economic participation

### City Ownership

- Zone management
- Population control
- Economic flow optimization

### Planet Ownership

- Resource distribution
- Rule definition
- Environmental control

### Solar System Ownership

- Multi-planet coordination
- Trade route control
- Expansion governance

## 7. World Lifecycle

Worlds are not static assets. They evolve over time.

Lifecycle flow:

Creation -> Early Life -> Civilization -> Expansion -> Maturity

User interaction points:

- Early stage: investment and speculation
- Mid stage: development and optimization
- Late stage: control and expansion

## 8. WaaS (World-as-a-Service) Layer

Sarakt allows external users and developers to create and extend worlds.

Capabilities:

- Launch custom planets
- Define unique resources and races
- Integrate with existing economy
- Build systems on top of Sarakt infrastructure

Developer access:

- APIs for land and asset management
- Economic system integration
- AI behavior modules

## 9. Business Model

Revenue streams:

- Initial Land Offering (ILO)
- Transaction fees
- World creation fees
- Premium modules and tools

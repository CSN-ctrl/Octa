Supabase → Authentication → Email templates

Paste the HTML from each .html file into the matching template in the dashboard.
Subjects (suggested):

  Confirm signup   → Confirm your Chaos Star account
  Reset password   → Reset your Sarakt password

Required: Authentication → URL configuration includes:
  https://www.sarakt.com/email-verified
  https://www.sarakt.com/reset-password
  http://localhost:5173/email-verified   (or your dev port)
  http://localhost:5173/reset-password

Links in templates must use href="{{ .ConfirmationURL }}" only (Supabase builds the full URL).

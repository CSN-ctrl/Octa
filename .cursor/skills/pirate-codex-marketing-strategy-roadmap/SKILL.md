# Pirate Codex Marketing - Strategy Roadmap Module

## Lessons
- Overview
- Where we AARRR
- Where the treasure lies
- How we get there
- Who is doing what
- Summary

## Workflow
1. Define north-star and stage metrics.
2. Map funnel stages to AARRR.
3. Identify bottleneck with highest leverage.
4. Plan milestones and experiments by stage.
5. Assign ownership and operating cadence.

## Checklist
- [ ] Stage metrics are explicit and measurable.
- [ ] AARRR mapping reflects current reality.
- [ ] Bottleneck choice is evidence-backed.
- [ ] Milestones have dates and owners.
- [ ] Summary includes risks and assumptions.

## Output Contract
Return:
1. Current lesson checkpoint
2. Strategy decisions and why
3. Metrics used for prioritization
4. Next lesson/task
